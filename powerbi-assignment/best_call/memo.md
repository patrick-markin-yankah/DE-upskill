# Monday calls, from last autumn

## What this intake can support

Last autumn can support three things on Monday morning.

- A call list for any week from 1 to 10, built only from what the trainers would have known by the end of that week.
- The board figures for this intake, by track: who graduated, who was certified, and who did not graduate.
- One page each for you, for a trainer, and for a learner, so a trainer does not spend the morning on the other track and a learner cannot read anyone else's name.

A new intake can be added in the same shape and the pages refreshed. A trainer of this intake will not see that new intake until they are assigned to it.

## What it cannot support

The call list does not find the people who fail. On this finished intake it names one Cloud learner, in weeks 4 and 5, and he did not graduate. The other 20 never appear. Everyone on the Power BI track stayed in class, so that track has no call at all.

The files record the ending as graduate or not, and certified or not. They do not record a day anyone left. A score of 0 is a real score. A blank score is missing work, and a missing average is not treated as under 50. This intake has no blank scores.

No class day was short enough to explain a room of absences. The shortest class still ran to a normal length. The one week that fell away was Cloud week 5, where the average quiz sat about 11 points below that track's other weeks.

Certification is a graduate who also passed the external exam. No learner was certified without graduating. The 27 who graduated and missed the exam are a separate follow-up from the Monday call. Their next step is exam preparation, not an attendance call.

## Who gets a call

The call list starts in week 4. A learner is on it when both of these are true:

- Attendance so far is under 60%.
- The average lab is under 50, or the average quiz is under 50.

One of those is not enough. Weeks 1 to 3 have no call. The list is everyone who qualifies. It is not cut off at 10 names.

A watch list sits under the call list, and it starts in week 1. A learner is on it when attendance so far is under 60%, or when both the average lab and the average quiz are under 50. One average under 50 is not enough for the watch list either. In week 1 the watch list holds 40 learners. That is not the Monday call. From week 4, anyone on the call list is already on the watch list.

## What a wrong call costs

A flagged learner is called that same week. A wrong flag costs a trainer's afternoon on someone who was fine.

Weeks 4 and 5 are one call, and that learner did not graduate. You asked for 10 right flags rather than 40 noisy ones. This list is 1 right and none wrong, and it misses 20 of the 21.

## Who may see what

- You see both tracks and every intake.
- A trainer sees only the track they own. The Cloud trainer does not see the Power BI call list. A trainer of this intake does not see the next intake until they are assigned to it.
- A learner sees only their own weeks, and how their quiz compares with their own track. That comparison has no classmate's name.
- Anyone who is none of these sees an empty page.

Someone who can change the report sees every row. The copy the team opens on Monday has to be view only, or the split above does not hold.

## Monday morning

Open the call page on week 4. The list is one name, John Miller on Cloud. Weeks 1 to 3 have no call. The Power BI track has no call in any week.

Use the page as the list of who looks thin enough to call. Do not use it as the list of who will fail. The watch list is the wider set, and it is not the call.
